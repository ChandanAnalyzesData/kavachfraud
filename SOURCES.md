# Sources

These are the reports and official pages each part of KavachFraud draws on. Links go to Indian newspapers, agencies, regulators and companies. The exceptions are the AI Incident Database page for IN-015, which republishes Indian press reports, and a few PressReader pages that mirror Indian newspapers.

Some links are older, and some may move or sit behind a paywall.

## Official portals and regulators

- [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (I4C, MHA)](https://cybercrime.gov.in): File a cyber complaint online and use the 1930 Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System.
- [RBI BE(A)WARE booklet](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/content/pdfs/BEAWARE07032022.pdf): RBI's compendium of modus operandi: SIM swap, QR scans, vishing, screen-sharing apps, lottery fraud.
- [DoT Sanchar Saathi and Chakshu](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in): Report suspected fraud calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages; check handset genuineness; block lost IMEIs.
- [SEBI alerts and registry](https://www.sebi.gov.in): Check whether an adviser, broker or portfolio manager is registered; see caution notices.
- [NPCI UPI safety](https://www.npci.org.in): UPI PIN boundaries, collect-request rules and AutoPay mandate guidance.
- [SEBI SCORES (investor complaints)](https://scores.sebi.gov.in): Lodge complaints against brokers, advisers and listed companies, and check SEBI-registered intermediaries.
- [RBI Complaint Management System](https://cms.rbi.org.in): Escalate unresolved complaints against banks, NBFCs and payment operators to the RBI Ombudsman.
- [RBI Sachet](https://sachet.rbi.org.in): Report unauthorised deposit schemes and check entities that are allowed to accept deposits.
- [CERT-In](https://www.cert-in.org.in): Report cyber incidents such as ransomware and phishing; organisations must report specified incidents within 6 hours.
- [PIB Fact Check](https://factcheck.pib.gov.in): Check viral messages that claim to be from the government, India Post, tax department or other agencies.
- [UIDAI (Aadhaar biometric lock)](https://uidai.gov.in): Lock and unlock Aadhaar biometrics to protect against cloned-fingerprint AePS fraud.
- [MEA eMigrate](https://emigrate.gov.in): Verify recruiting agents and overseas employers before accepting a job abroad.
- [National Consumer Helpline (1915)](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in): Consumer complaints about fake online stores, non-delivery and wrong or useless goods.
